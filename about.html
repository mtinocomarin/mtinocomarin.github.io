About Milton

Curious about the whole system.

I am an Electrical and Computer Engineering master's student, researcher, and laboratory instructor at San Francisco State University. I build across hardware and software to turn sensing, computation, and physical interaction into useful technology.

My path

From microcontrollers to human-centered systems.

I first became interested in engineering through an embedded systems course, where I discovered how much I enjoyed microcontrollers and low-level programming. Building projects and gaining research experience grew that interest into a focus on robotics and assistive technology.

Today, I am completing an M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering after earning my B.S. in Computer Engineering from San Francisco State University. My current work combines embedded control, EMG signal processing, machine learning, and computer vision to develop assistive systems with practical human impact.

As a first-generation college student, I value the people who supported me throughout my education. That experience shapes how I teach and mentor undergraduate students: I want technical spaces to feel challenging, collaborative, and possible.
