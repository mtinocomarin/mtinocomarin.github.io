About Milton

Curious about the whole system.

I work across hardware and software to understand how sensing, computation, and physical interaction can become useful technology.

How I work

I enjoy moving between the detail of low-level firmware and the broader system: selecting sensors, designing real-time pipelines, evaluating intelligent algorithms, and communicating the result clearly.

What drives me

My strongest interests sit where technical depth meets human impact—assistive robotics, biomedical signals, wearable systems, perception, and tools that help people understand or extend their capabilities.
