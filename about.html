About Milton / Bay Area engineer

Embedded Systems, Robotics & Mentorship

I am an embedded-systems researcher, robotics engineer, laboratory instructor, and mentor focused on technology that can make a meaningful difference.

My path

From the Bay Area to embedded research.

I grew up in the Bay Area and studied Computer Engineering at San Francisco State University. During my undergraduate years, I began conducting research and concentrated on building real-time, bare-metal embedded systems. That work taught me how to move from an idea to functioning hardware through low-level firmware, careful debugging, and hands-on experimentation.

In the Electrical and Computer Engineering master's program at SFSU, I have been expanding that foundation into robotics. My work now brings embedded control together with machine learning, computer vision, biosignals, and assistive technology.

I also develop research software and analysis tools, including programs for experimental analysis, hardware interfaces, real-time data visualization, and file management. I enjoy building the supporting tools that make experiments more reliable, repeatable, and easier for a research team to use.

Teaching is an important part of who I am. Alongside my research, I work as a laboratory instructor and mentor. I currently help lead a research team that includes two undergraduate students, supporting their technical development while we work together on challenging engineering problems.

Looking ahead, I want to continue contributing to community-centered research in assistive technology and join teams working on ambitious systems with meaningful real-world impact.
